Application Overview

The Municipal Public Meetings Portal is a comprehensive application designed to aggregate, organize, and display public meeting schedules across Palm Beach County municipalities. It scrapes and monitors official municipal websites to provide residents, officials, and stakeholders with a centralized, searchable interface for discovering upcoming governance meetings and accessing related agendas.

This tool enables transparency and civic engagement by consolidating meeting information that would otherwise be scattered across multiple municipal websites.

Keywords & Search Terms

The following keywords are used to identify and categorize relevant meeting types. These terms are matched against meeting titles, agendas, and summaries to help users discover meetings by topic or governance area:

Zoning Zoning board meetings, variance requests, land use decisions
CRA Community Redevelopment Agency meetings
Commission City/County commission and council meetings
Planning Planning board and development meetings
Budget Financial and budget-related meetings
Board General board meetings across departments
